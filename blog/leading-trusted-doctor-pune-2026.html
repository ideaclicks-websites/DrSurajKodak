Leading & Trusted Doctor in Pune – Awarded 2026

Dr. Suraj Avinash KodakSeptember 5, 20264 min read

Being named a "Leading & Trusted Doctor in Pune" is not a title that is awarded lightly. For Dr. Suraj Avinash Kodak of Medicos Clinic, Vishrantwadi, the recognition at the Maharashtra Excellence Awards & Conclave 2026 reflects a combination of formal qualifications, sustained patient outcomes and more than a decade of clinical practice.

The Qualifications Behind the Title

  • MBBS — foundational medical qualification
  • MD (Internal Medicine) — advanced specialisation in diagnosis and management of adult medical conditions
  • CCRJD — certified training in Rheumatology
  • PG Diploma in Diabetology & Clinical Training — focused diabetes management expertise

This academic foundation, combined with 10+ years of clinical experience, forms the basis of the "leading doctor" recognition — qualifications alone rarely earn patient trust, but they set the standard that ongoing practice is then measured against.

The Patient Outcomes Behind the Trust

Recognition as a trusted doctor ultimately depends on what happens in the consultation room, year after year. Medicos Clinic's 4.9★ rating from 244+ patient reviews reflects consistent experiences across diabetes management, rheumatoid arthritis treatment, thyroid care and general internal medicine consultations — including patients who travel from outside Pune specifically to continue care with Dr. Kodak.

"We highly recommend Medicos and Dr. Suraj to anyone seeking reliable and effective treatment." — Gayatri Gulaskar, patient feedback

The 2026 Recognition

In August 2026, this combination of qualification and consistent patient care was formally recognised at the Maharashtra Excellence Awards & Conclave 2026, where Dr. Kodak was honoured as the Leading & Trusted Physician in Rheumatology, Diabetes & Internal Medicine Care for the year.

For patients across Vishrantwadi, Dhanori, Lohgaon, Viman Nagar and Koregaon Park, this 2026 recognition is a reminder that the care available locally at Medicos Clinic meets a standard acknowledged well beyond the neighbourhood clinic.
