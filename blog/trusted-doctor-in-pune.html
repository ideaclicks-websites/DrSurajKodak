Trusted Doctor in Pune – Dr. Suraj Avinash Kodak

Dr. Suraj Avinash KodakSeptember 16, 20264 min read

Trust is not something a clinic can advertise into existence — it is built one consultation at a time. For Dr. Suraj Avinash Kodak at Medicos Clinic, Vishrantwadi, that trust now shows up in two places: in the words of his patients, and in the recognition of his peers.

What Builds Trust in a Long-Term Doctor–Patient Relationship

Conditions such as diabetes, thyroid disorders and rheumatoid arthritis are rarely resolved in a single visit. They require years of monitoring, adjustment and honest conversation. Patients who have consulted Dr. Kodak for years — some travelling from outside Pune for follow-up care — describe a few recurring themes in their feedback:

  • Clear explanation of the condition and the reasoning behind each treatment step
  • Availability for questions between visits, including over WhatsApp
  • A calm, unhurried approach even for complex or long-standing conditions
  • Consistency — the same physician, the same clinic, over many years of follow-up
"He treats his patients like family. He takes the time to truly understand the problem and provides the right guidance." — Kiran Birambole, patient feedback

Reflected in Patient Reviews

Medicos Clinic currently holds a 4.9★ rating from 244+ Google reviews, many of which specifically mention long-term management of rheumatoid arthritis, diabetes and family care spanning several years. You can read more patient experiences in the reviews section of the clinic website.

Trust, Recognised Externally

In August 2026, this pattern of patient trust was formally recognised when Dr. Kodak received the Maharashtra Excellence Award 2026, naming him a Leading & Trusted Physician in Rheumatology, Diabetes and Internal Medicine Care. For a physician, that kind of recognition typically follows — rather than creates — patient trust, and in Dr. Kodak's case, it reflects years of consistent, attentive care already familiar to his patients in Vishrantwadi, Dhanori and Lohgaon.
